Restore Purchases

This page explains what the Restore option does in ToonVa and how to recover an active ToonVa Pro subscription purchased through the Apple App Store.

Privacy Policy Support Email Support

Restore does not create a new purchase. It checks your Apple App Store purchase history and reconnects an existing active ToonVa Pro subscription to your ToonVa account.

When to Use Restore

  • You bought ToonVa Pro but Pro credits did not appear.
  • You reinstalled ToonVa.
  • You changed devices.
  • You signed in again and need to reconnect your subscription.
  • The app cannot detect your active subscription automatically.

How to Restore

  1. Open ToonVa on your iPhone.
  2. Sign in with the same Apple account used for the purchase.
  3. Open the paywall or subscription screen.
  4. Tap Restore.
  5. Wait for ToonVa to refresh your purchase status.

What Restore Does

Restore checks whether your Apple account has an active ToonVa Pro purchase. If an active Pro entitlement is found, ToonVa may activate Pro status and refresh the Pro credit package for your account.

What Restore Does Not Do

  • It does not charge you again.
  • It does not start a new subscription.
  • It does not cancel your subscription.
  • It does not refund purchases.

Canceling a Subscription

To cancel ToonVa Pro, manage your subscription through Apple:

  1. Open iPhone Settings.
  2. Tap your Apple ID.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Select ToonVa.
  5. Choose cancel or manage subscription.
