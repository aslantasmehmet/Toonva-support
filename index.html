This Privacy Policy explains how ToonVa collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use our mobile application, services, subscriptions, AI cartoon generation features, and related support channels.

ToonVa lets users upload photos and generate cartoon-style avatars, caricatures, profile images, and creative outputs using AI-powered image processing. Because the Service may involve personal photos, account information, purchase status, and generated images, we treat privacy and security as core parts of the product.

By using ToonVa, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you sign in or create an account, we may collect:

  • Your user identifier and account status.
  • Your email address, if provided by Apple Sign in.
  • Your Apple account authentication identifier or related login token metadata.
  • Subscription or Pro status, including whether your account has an active entitlement.
  • Credit balances, including preview credits and HD credits.
  • Account timestamps, such as creation date, last sign-in date, and update date.

We use Apple Sign in for authentication. Apple may provide a private relay email address instead of your real email address depending on your Apple account settings.

Photos and Uploaded Images

To use ToonVa’s core features, you may choose to upload or select photos from your device, including selfies, portrait photos, source images used for cartoon generation, and images captured through your camera if camera access is enabled.

We only access photos or camera input when you grant permission through your device settings or the operating system permission prompt. ToonVa does not scan your entire photo library.

Generated Images and Creative Outputs

When you generate a cartoon or avatar, we may store:

  • The generated cartoon image.
  • The source image path or reference.
  • The generated result path or reference.
  • Generation prompt metadata.
  • Selected style, filter, frame, or creative effect.
  • Generation status, provider, model, and technical metadata.
  • Creation date and update date.

Purchase and Subscription Information

ToonVa uses RevenueCat to manage in-app purchases and subscriptions. We may receive and store whether your purchase was successful, your active entitlement, product identifier, offering or package information, subscription status, restore purchase status, RevenueCat customer identifier, and the app user identifier linked to your ToonVa account.

We do not directly collect or store your full payment card number. Payments are processed by Apple App Store, RevenueCat, or other authorized payment infrastructure depending on platform and availability.

Usage, Device, and Support Information

We may collect usage and technical information such as features used, styles selected, number of generations attempted, credit usage, errors, app version, device type, operating system, language settings, crash logs, diagnostics, support messages, screenshots you choose to send, and approximate network metadata required to operate the Service.

2. How We Use Information

  • To create and manage your ToonVa account.
  • To let you sign in securely.
  • To upload your selected photos.
  • To generate cartoon images and avatars.
  • To save generated images to your account.
  • To track preview credits and HD credits.
  • To enable paid Pro features and restore purchases.
  • To process AI image generation using your selected image and style instructions.
  • To prevent fraud, spam, abuse, and excessive automated usage.
  • To debug errors, improve app performance, and respond to support requests.

3. Legal Bases for Processing

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we process personal information based on contract, consent, legitimate interests, and legal obligations. These bases allow us to provide the Service, process user-selected photos, manage purchases, protect our systems, and comply with tax, accounting, consumer protection, app store, and legal requirements.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers that help operate ToonVa, including authentication providers, cloud database and storage providers, AI generation infrastructure providers, payment and subscription management providers, analytics or diagnostic providers, and customer support tools.

We may disclose information if reasonably necessary to comply with law, enforce our terms, detect or prevent fraud, address security issues, or protect the rights, property, or safety of ToonVa, users, or others.

5. Data Retention

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. Account information is kept while your account is active; generated images are kept while saved in your account; purchase records may be retained for subscription management, tax, accounting, dispute resolution, and platform compliance; support messages may be retained to resolve requests and maintain support history.

6. Account Deletion

You may request account deletion from within the app where available or by contacting support. When account deletion is processed, your ToonVa account is removed or disabled, saved creations associated with your account may be deleted, and account-linked records may be deleted or de-identified.

Deleting your ToonVa account does not automatically cancel your App Store subscription. You must manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple account settings.

7. Your Choices and Controls

  • You can control photo library and camera permissions through your device settings.
  • You may update or delete your account through available app settings or by contacting us.
  • Where available, you may delete saved creations from within the app.
  • You can manage subscriptions through your Apple App Store account settings.
