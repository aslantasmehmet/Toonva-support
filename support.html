Common Support Topics

Account and Sign In

If you cannot sign in, make sure:

  • You are using the same Apple account previously connected to ToonVa.
  • Apple Sign in is available on your device.
  • Your internet connection is active.
  • ToonVa is updated to the latest available version.

If the issue continues, contact us at support@venby.co.

Purchases and Subscriptions

ToonVa subscriptions are managed through Apple App Store purchases. If you purchased ToonVa Pro but credits did not appear:

  • Open ToonVa.
  • Go to the paywall or subscription screen.
  • Tap Restore.
  • Wait for the app to refresh your purchase status.

If your purchase still does not appear, contact support with your ToonVa account email and the approximate purchase time.

To cancel a subscription, manage it from your Apple account:

  • Open iPhone Settings.
  • Tap your Apple ID.
  • Tap Subscriptions.
  • Select ToonVa.
  • Choose cancel or manage subscription.

ToonVa cannot directly cancel App Store subscriptions on your behalf.

Credits and Limits

ToonVa uses credits for AI generation:

  • Preview credits are used for standard cartoon generations.
  • HD credits are used for higher quality or VIP+ generations.
  • Pro plans may include weekly preview and HD credits.

If credits look incorrect, try closing and reopening the app. If they still look wrong, contact support.

AI Cartoon Generation

If a generation fails:

  • Check your internet connection.
  • Try another photo.
  • Make sure the image is clear and not too dark.
  • Avoid screenshots, heavily edited images, or very low-resolution photos.
  • Try again later if the service is busy.

Credits should only be deducted after a successful generation. If you believe credits were deducted incorrectly, contact support.

Photo Library and Camera Permissions

ToonVa needs photo access so you can select images to cartoon. If upload is not working:

  • Open iPhone Settings.
  • Find ToonVa.
  • Enable Photos or Camera permission.

You can change these permissions anytime from your device settings.

Saved Creations

If your creations are missing:

  • Make sure you are signed in with the correct Apple account.
  • Check your internet connection.
  • Refresh the creations screen.
  • Contact support if the issue continues.

Account Deletion

If you want to delete your ToonVa account, use the in-app account deletion option where available or contact us at support@venby.co.

Deleting your ToonVa account does not automatically cancel your App Store subscription. You must cancel active subscriptions through your Apple account settings.

Privacy Requests

For privacy-related requests, including account data access, correction, deletion, or other privacy rights, contact support@venby.co. Please include enough information for us to verify and locate your account.

Response Times

We aim to respond to support requests as soon as possible. Response times may vary depending on request volume, weekends, holidays, technical complexity, and whether we need additional information from you.

Abuse and Safety

ToonVa may restrict accounts or generation requests that violate our policies, abuse the Service, attempt to bypass limits, upload unlawful content, or interfere with normal app operation. If you believe your account was restricted by mistake, contact support with details.
